# Synthesize evidence

Read [GOAL](../../../GOAL.md), [CAMPAIGN](../../../CAMPAIGN.md), [BOOKKEEPING](../../../BOOKKEEPING.md), [FINDINGS](../../../FINDINGS.md), relevant campaign protocols and retained results. Apply [the finding criteria](../../../campaigns/TEMPLATE.md) and [standing rules](../../../AGENTS.md).

Separate observations from interpretation and proposals. Check controls, uncertainty, independent units, replication, tested scale, and surviving alternatives. Qualify mismatched protocols and historical comparisons instead of merging them into a stronger claim. Do not tune away negatives or replace a preregistered verdict with a post-hoc success rule.

Update the current campaign RESULTS with execution state, scientific verdict, key evidence, limitations, spend, and next action. Keep history in RUN_LOG/root LOG. Rewrite BOOKKEEPING around live implications; promote only adequately supported, plan-changing/publication-relevant evidence to FINDINGS using the contract's fields. When none qualifies, retain its exact empty-state line.

Preserve the original goal. A requested scope change gets a dated revision and evidence or user-direction attribution; an agent's preference is not a new goal. Put detailed synthesis in the campaign, keeping root files within their limits.

Use existing evidence. New model scoring, training, or additional experiments require their own authorized scope and budget. Report what is resolved, what only receives evidence, and what would change the next decision.
