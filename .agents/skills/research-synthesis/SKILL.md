# Synthesize evidence

Read the goal, campaign index, bookkeeping, findings, frozen protocols, and retained results. Apply [finding criteria](../../../campaigns/TEMPLATE.md) and [standing rules](../../../AGENTS.md).

- Separate observation, interpretation, and proposal. Check controls, uncertainty, independent units, replication, tested scale, and alternatives.
- Qualify mismatched comparisons. Preserve negatives and frozen verdicts; label post-hoc analysis.
- Update RESULTS and live implications in BOOKKEEPING. Promote only qualifying evidence to FINDINGS; otherwise keep its empty-state line. Keep detail/history in campaign records and logs.
- Preserve the original goal; append user-directed changes with evidence or explicit user-direction attribution.

Use existing evidence; new experiments need their own authorization and budget. Report what is resolved, what remains uncertain, and what changes the next decision.
